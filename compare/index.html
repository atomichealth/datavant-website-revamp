Applied UI/UX improvements

Same content and brand — better hierarchy, rhythm and consistency.

  1. 1:1 contentSame copy, nav, sections, resources and footer as the live site — only the presentation changes: hierarchy, color, spacing and micro-interactions.
  2. Typographic hierarchyConsistent scale (display → h2 → h3 → lede) with a single section pattern: eyebrow + title + lede. The original mixes multiple H1s and uneven sizes.
  3. CTA hierarchy"Learn More" as the primary action and "Contact Us" as secondary in the hero; the brand cyan CTA reserved for the nav bar and the closing banner.
  4. Intentional colorBrand cyan #14e7e8 and blue #475ff2 are reserved for accents and actions; navy for emphasis sections. Less noise, more hierarchical contrast.
  5. Logos: clean marqueeInfinite grayscale carousel with color on hover. Bug fixed: the live site shows the placeholder "Add a short supporting paragraph here".
  6. Who We Serve in navyAll 5 audiences in uniform cards with icons, equal heights and a consistent "Read more" affordance — the original mixes layouts.
  7. Stats as their own moduleBrand-gradient figures on white cards; previously they competed visually with the AI features block.
  8. Uniform product grid6 cards with category tags, same structure and hover elevation; the arrow link signals interactivity.
  9. Scannable resourcesCards with category + date first, truncatable titles and short excerpts instead of long paragraphs.
  10. Spacing rhythm8pt system: 104px sections, 22-24px gaps, 16px radii across all modules.
  11. Micro-interactionsSubtle reveal-on-scroll, hover lift on cards and animated link arrows — feedback without distraction.
